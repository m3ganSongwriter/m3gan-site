Welcome

Welcome to my official music hub.

I launched my pop music career back in 2022, and this website serves as my direct, unfiltered archive for everything that I write and release as a pop singer-songwriter.

Instead of relying on social media platforms, I will document my entire creative process right here under one roof. You can explore the core sections of my platform below:

  • Behind the Songs

    Timelines, Studio Logs, and Breakdown Stories
    This section documents the actual studio history and tracking timelines behind my song releases.

This track is critical not because it was my first ever release, but because its launch on February 28, 2022, officially timestamps my stage name—months before the doll movie had its premiere on December 7, 2022, and used my name without my consent.

  • Songwriting Tips

    Notes, Workflows, and Hooks
    This section serves as a direct notebook, containing the steps and lyrical structures I use when songwriting.

    When writing songs I concentrate on the storytelling side first. Then I start to remove, and format the lyrics to form the song structure. I map out the intro, verses, pre-chorus, chorus, drops, hooks, bridges, interludes and finally the outro.

    Everything here is stripped of generic, textbook advice. These are my real workflow logs detailing how I arrange my vocal layers, and introduce the musical aspect.

    Playlists are crucial for artists because they give new releases instant traction. Instead of waiting for slow organic reach, getting a new track placed on the right playlist pushes it directly to active listeners the moment it drops, building immediate streaming momentum.
