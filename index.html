Songwriting Tips

Notes, Workflows, and Hooks

This section serves as a direct notebook, containing the steps and lyrical structures I use when songwriting.

When writing songs I concentrate on the storytelling side first. Then I start to remove, and format the lyrics to form the song structure. I map out the intro, verses, pre-chorus, chorus, drops, hooks, bridges, interludes and finally the outro.

Everything here is stripped of generic, textbook advice. These are my real workflow logs detailing how I arrange my vocal layers, and introduce the musical aspect.
