Welcome

This is my official central hub. I launched this pop music catalog in 2022, and this website serves as the direct, unfiltered archive for everything I write and release as a pop singer-songwriter.

Instead of relying on fragmented social media platforms, I document my entire creative process right here under one roof. You can explore the core branches of this platform below:

  • Behind the Songs

    The real timelines, studio logs, and breakdown stories behind my track releases.

  • Playlists

    Curated song lists and specific track combinations tracking my musical influences.

  • Songwriting Tips

    My personal notes, practical workflows, and exercises for writing lyrics and hooks.
